Returning customers didn't just come back — most of them stayed.

Of 81 customers active in 1998, 13 went quiet for six months or more and then placed a new order. This report tracks what happened after they came back: how strong the comeback was, whether it lasted, and what they bought.

Final takeaway

The strongest business signal isn't whether a customer returns — it's whether they keep buying afterward. Reactivation campaigns built around staple, repeat-purchase categories like Beverages and Dairy look like the highest-leverage lever here.
